freeXplorer is a free alternative to fatXplorer that lets you open, browse, and manage the contents of Xbox-formatted storage drives on your Windows computer. ## ✨ What Is freeXplorer?

Modern Windows PCs are built to read NTFS and exFAT drives. Xbox consoles use their own storage layout, which means a drive that works perfectly on your console can appear completely empty or unreadable when you plug it into a laptop. freeXplorer bridges that gap. It reads the console storage format and presents your folders and files in a familiar window, so you can copy things out, copy things in, and tidy up your drive the same way you would with any other folder on your PC.

The project exists because the most common tools for this job sit behind a paywall. freeXplorer is the community-minded answer: the same core idea, offered at no cost, aimed at people who just want to move a few save files or back up a drive.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**The app opens but my drive is not listed.** Unplug the drive and plug it back in, then refresh the drive list. Try a different USB port, ideally one directly on the computer rather than a hub.

**Windows does not react when I plug the drive in.** Confirm the cable is fully seated at both ends. Some enclosures need their own power supply, so check that it is switched on.

**A copy operation stopped partway through.** Check that there is enough free space on the destination, then try again with a single file to confirm the connection is stable.

**Windows blocked the file from running.** Open the file's properties and look for an option to allow it, then try again. Security prompts are normal for newly downloaded software.
